Smarti Board is an infinite idea board for strategy and product work, with a built-in Smarti Assistant that points out the missing risk, the unspoken connection — one suggestion at a time. Nothing reaches the board until you accept it, and any board can be marked private so it never reaches a model at all.

The trust model

You always know whose idea is whose.

Suggestions live in their own layer, so it's never ambiguous what's yours and what's proposed. Three visually distinct layers coexist on the board at all times.

There is no code path that merges a suggestion into your content implicitly. Accepting constructs a brand-new node and discards the proposal — one action, reversible with ⌘Z.

The Smarti Assistant

It points out what the board is missing.

As you work, the assistant reads the whole graph — never per keystroke — and offers one suggestion at a time:

  • missing risks
  • unaddressed segments
  • unstated assumptions
  • absent success metrics
  • hidden dependencies

Each arrives with its reasoning, one click from accepted or gone. Placement spirals outward from the ideas it references, taking the first spot that occludes nothing you placed.

When it runs

  1. Privacy Mode first — a private board is never told to a model at all.
  2. 4 seconds of quiet after your last meaningful change.
  3. A fingerprint of text and topology — dragging a card never spends a token.
  4. At least three ideas before there's anything to reason about.
  5. One live suggestion on the canvas at a time.
  6. It remembers what you dismissed this session. No re-offers, reworded.

A suggestion appearing is never in your undo stack. Accepting is — reversed, the board would feel haunted.

When you ask

Or just ask for ideas.

⌘ . opens the ideas panel — the one behavior you invoke rather than wait for. One click asks for a handful of candidates: for the whole board, or branching off the card you have selected. They stream in with their reasons and stage in the panel until you add the ones that land.

Add is the only bridge to the canvas. Adding builds a fresh node, wires it to the ideas it came from, and goes into your undo stack like any other edit.

It streams — ideas appear one at a time as the model writes them · the list is cached while the board is unchanged, so reopening spends nothing · an objective alone on an empty board is enough to start.

Stay in control

Point it at what matters. Or wall it off.

⌘ J

Every board can say what it's for.

An objective is a few lines — up to 400 characters — on what this board is trying to do. It leads both prompts, so suggestions stop being generic gap-fills and start being about your actual stakes. It changes what the AI says, never when.

And it stays yours: nothing rewrites, condenses, or restates your objective back at you.

"Find the three things worth shipping before October — and the risk each one is quietly carrying."
⌘ ⇧ P

Some boards are walls.

Privacy Mode is per board. While it's on, nothing in that board is sent to any model — the routes refuse on the server, not just in the tab, and ⌘Z can never turn it off, because an undo that quietly reopened the door is one you'd never notice.

Your key stays configured; every other board keeps its suggestions as before.

Bring your own model

You choose who answers.

Pick a provider in settings; change your mind any time. The board works the same either way — the assistant is simply on or off.

Anthropic

Claude — the reference path. Structured output, adaptive thinking.

z.ai

GLM models over the OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

z.ai Coding Plan

For Coding Plan subscriptions, over the Anthropic-compatible endpoint.

Ollama

Fully local. Nothing leaves your machine.

Custom

Anything speaking OpenAI chat-completions: LM Studio, vLLM, OpenRouter.

The key is stored server-side in the same SQLite file as your boards — write-only, never returned to the browser past the last four characters. And configuring nothing is a supported configuration: the board is fully usable; the assistant simply stays off.

The board itself

Worth using without the AI.

The assistant is optional, but the board underneath is no demo. Local interactions never wait on a model — ever.

Instant autosave

Every keystroke lands in SQLite on its own. There is no save button anywhere in the app.

Boards name themselves

Titled after the first idea. Rename it and the override sticks; clear it and the name follows the content again.

Undo and redo

⌘Z walks back edits — accepting a suggestion included. A suggestion appearing never enters the stack.

Multi-select

Shift-click or sweep a marquee; move the set, or delete it all as one undoable step.

Presentation mode

⌘⇧F puts the board on a screen for a room: fullscreen, read-only, everything fitted to view.

Done marking

Cross an idea off with ✓ — still on the board, clearly finished, and the assistant knows what's done.

Rich text, five colors

Bold, italic, underline, strike and a fixed palette — formatting never reaches the prompt.

Size it your way

Resize any card; five text sizes on a fixed ladder. Presentation, never content — the model sees none of it.

Self-hosting

Yours to run.

One process, one SQLite file, no external services. The database is created on first run — no setup step, nothing to seed, no accounts.

  • start.sh checks your Node (22–26) and, if it can't be used, downloads its own into ./.node — no sudo, no version manager, nothing outside the cloned folder.
  • Your boards and your API key live in one file you can back up, move, or delete. SMARTI_DB_PATH if you want it elsewhere.
  • Works fully offline with Ollama — the assistant included.

AGPL-3.0-only. Self-host it freely; if you run a modified version as a network service, the source goes back out.
